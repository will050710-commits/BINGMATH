Since PT is perpendicular to AB passing through the midpoint of AB, we have AT = TB.

In right triangle PAT, using Pythagorean theorem:
PA^2 + AT^2 = PT^2

Substituting given values:
(8)^2 + AT^3 = (12)^2
64 + AT^2= 144
AT^2 = 70
AT = √70

Therefore, RT = OA - AT = OT - AT = OR - AT = RT - AT = 5√7.

Thus, the length of segment RT is 5√7 units.

Answer: The value of RT is $\boxed{5\sqrt{7}}$.
